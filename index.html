Free CSV to Chart Generator

iqplotter turns CSV and Excel files into bar, line, scatter and pie charts in seconds. Everything runs in your browser, so your data is never uploaded to any server.

How it works

  1. Drag and drop a CSV, TSV, or Excel (.xlsx) file, or paste raw tabular text.
  2. Pick the columns for your X and Y axes.
  3. Choose a chart type (Bar, Line, Scatter, or Pie) and export it as PNG or SVG.

Features

  • Interactive bar, line, scatter, and pie charts
  • Automatic data cleaning, currency scrubbing, and type detection
  • 100% private client-side processing — zero server uploads
  • High-resolution PNG and vector SVG export
  • Dark mode and accessible high-contrast palettes
  • Free to use with no account or sign-up required

Frequently asked questions

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Files are processed locally in your browser memory. Your data never leaves your computer.

Which file types are supported?

CSV, TSV, plain-text delimited tables, and Excel (.xlsx, .xls) files are supported.

Is iqplotter free?

Yes, 100% free with no sign-up or subscription required.

Does iqplotter work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, all file parsing and charting run offline in your browser.
