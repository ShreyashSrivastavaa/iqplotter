Free CSV to Chart Generator

iqplotter turns CSV and Excel files into bar, line, scatter and pie charts in seconds. Everything runs in your browser, so your data is never uploaded.

How it works

  1. Drag and drop a CSV or Excel file.
  2. Pick the columns for your X and Y axes.
  3. Choose a chart type and export it as an image.

Features

  • Bar, line, scatter and pie charts
  • Automatic data cleaning and type detection
  • Dark mode
  • No account, no upload, free to use

Frequently asked questions

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Files are processed locally in your browser.

Which file types are supported?

CSV, TSV and Excel (.xlsx) files.

Is iqplotter free?

Yes, with no sign-up required.
